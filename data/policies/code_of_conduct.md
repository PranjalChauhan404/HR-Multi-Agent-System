# Code of Conduct

## Professional Behaviour
All employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully toward colleagues, managers, customers, and business partners.

## Respectful Workplace
Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and abusive behaviour are not acceptable. Employees should report workplace concerns to HR.

## Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company information, employee records, and business data. Such information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

## Responsible Use of Company Resources
Company devices, accounts, software, and other resources should be used responsibly and in accordance with company guidelines.

## Reporting Violations
Employees may report suspected violations to HR. Reports should be handled confidentially and reviewed by an authorized person.

## Important Note
This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration. It does not represent a real company's employment policy.
