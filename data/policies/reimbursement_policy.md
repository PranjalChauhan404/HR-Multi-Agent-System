# Reimbursement Policy

**Note:** This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration. It does not represent a real company's employment policy.

## 1. Eligible Expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business-related expenses, including:

* Business travel and transportation.
* Accommodation during approved business trips.
* Work-related purchases and office supplies.
* Client meetings and business meals.
* Other expenses explicitly approved by management.

## 2. Reimbursement Limits

The following limits apply under this mock policy:

* Local business transportation: Up to ₹3,000 per month.
* Accommodation: Up to ₹4,000 per night.
* Office supplies: Up to ₹2,000 per purchase.
* Business meals: Up to ₹1,500 per meeting.
* Expenses exceeding these limits require special management approval.

## 3. Submission Requirements

Employees must submit a reimbursement request containing:

* Expense amount.
* Expense date.
* Expense category.
* Business purpose.
* Supporting receipts or invoices, where applicable.

Requests should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense.

## 4. Approval Process

* Reimbursement requests must be reviewed and approved by an authorized manager or HR representative before payment.
* Expenses exceeding standard limits require additional management approval.
* Employees cannot approve their own reimbursement requests.
* Requests remain pending until an authorized person records a decision.

## 5. Ineligible Expenses

The following expenses are not eligible:

* Personal purchases.
* Expenses unrelated to approved business activities.
* Alcohol and entertainment expenses without prior authorization.
* Expenses without sufficient supporting documentation.

## 6. Missing Documentation

Requests with missing receipts or insufficient details may be returned to the employee for clarification.

Employees must provide the missing information before the request can proceed.

## 7. Processing

Approved reimbursements are processed according to the company's finance procedures.

The HR assistant must not promise a specific payment date unless verified through an authorized system.

## 8. Important Note

This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration. It does not represent a real company's employment policy.
