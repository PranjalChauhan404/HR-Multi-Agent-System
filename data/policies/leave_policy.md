# Employee Leave Policy

**Note:** This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration. It does not represent a real company's employment policy.

## 1. Casual Leave

* Employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year.
* Casual leave is intended for personal matters and short absences.
* Employees should submit requests before taking leave whenever possible.
* Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.

## 2. Sick Leave

* Employees receive 10 sick leave days per calendar year.
* Employees should notify their manager when illness prevents them from working.
* HR may request supporting documentation when appropriate.
* Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.

## 3. Earned Leave

* Employees receive 15 earned leave days per calendar year.
* Earned leave should be requested in advance and requires manager approval.
* Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused earned leave days to the next calendar year.
* Earned leave beyond the carry-forward limit expires at year-end.
* Earned leave encashment is not available under this mock policy.

## 4. Leave Application

* All leave requests must be submitted through the HR system.
* Planned leave should be requested at least 3 working days in advance.
* Emergency sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.
* Employees must provide the leave type, start date and end date.
* Requests remain pending until an authorized manager approves or rejects them.
* Employees must not consider leave approved until approval is recorded in the HR system.

## 5. Leave Approval

* All leave requests require manager approval.
* Employees cannot approve their own leave requests.
* Managers may approve or reject requests based on available leave balance and business requirements.
* Requests exceeding the available balance must be rejected.

## 6. Leave Cancellation

* Employees may request cancellation of previously approved leave through the HR system.
* Cancellation requires manager approval.
* Leave balances are updated after cancellation is approved.

## 7. Leave Balance

* Leave availability depends on the employee's recorded balance.
* A request exceeding the available balance must be rejected.
* Employees can contact HR if they notice discrepancies in their leave balance.

## 8. Leave Carry-Forward and Expiry

* Up to 5 unused Earned Leave days may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
* Casual Leave and Sick Leave cannot be carried forward.
* Unused Casual Leave and Sick Leave expire at the end of the calendar year.
* Earned Leave exceeding the carry-forward limit also expires at year-end.

## 9. Important Note

This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration.

It does not represent a real company's employment policy.
