## Employee Onboarding Policy

Every new employee must complete the onboarding process.

Required Documents
Government-issued identity proof.
Address proof.
Educational certificates.
Previous employment documents, where applicable.
Bank account details.
Passport-size photographs.
Onboarding Checklist

## Before Joining

HR sends the offer letter.
Employee submits required documents.
IT prepares laptop and system access.

## First Day

HR completes document verification.
Employee receives company policies.
IT provides login credentials and equipment.
Manager introduces the employee to the team.

## First Week

Employee completes mandatory training.
Manager explains responsibilities and expectations.
Employee receives access to relevant tools and systems.

## First Month

Manager conducts an initial performance discussion.
HR checks onboarding completion.
Employee provides onboarding feedback.
