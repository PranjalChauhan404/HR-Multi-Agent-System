# Work From Home Policy

**Note:** This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration. It does not represent a real company's employment policy.

## 1. Eligibility

* Work-from-home arrangements depend on job responsibilities and approval from the employee's manager.
* Employees whose roles require physical presence may not be eligible.
* New employees must complete their first 30 working days before requesting regular remote-work arrangements.

## 2. Requesting Remote Work

* Employees should submit work-from-home requests through the designated HR system or communicate with their manager according to company procedures.
* Planned remote-work requests should be submitted at least one working day in advance.
* Employees should specify the requested date and reason.

## 3. Approval

* Managers are responsible for reviewing work-from-home requests.
* A request should not be treated as approved until authorization is recorded.
* Approval depends on team requirements and business operations.
* Employees must not assume approval based on a pending request.

## 4. Availability and Communication

* Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during their agreed working hours.
* Employees must attend required meetings.
* Employees should remain reachable through approved communication channels.
* Any unexpected unavailability should be communicated to their manager.

## 5. Data Security

* Employees must protect company information when working remotely.
* Only authorized devices, accounts and systems may be used.
* Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.
* Employees must follow applicable company security procedures.

## 6. Changes to Arrangements

* Managers may review or modify remote-work arrangements according to business requirements and applicable company procedures.
* Previously approved arrangements may be withdrawn when business needs require it.
* Employees should coordinate changes with their managers.

## 7. Important Note

This is a fictional policy created for the HR Agent demonstration. It does not represent a real company's employment policy.
